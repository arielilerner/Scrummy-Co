Mornings with a toddler are beautiful, chaotic, and absolutely covered in porridge. You want to feed them something nutritious, but you're also half-dressed, late, and running on very little sleep.

Scrummy & Co. started with one knackered mum's simple belief: that the healthiest option should also be the easiest one. Our oat pancakes are made from ingredients you'd actually recognise: oats, real fruit, eggs, whole wheat flour. No additives, no sweeteners, no compromise.

They're soft, fluffy, and on their plate in 40 seconds. Little ones can feed themselves, the floor stays clean, and you might even get to drink your coffee hot.
